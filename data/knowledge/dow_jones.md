# Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA)

The Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA) is one of the oldest and most widely recognized stock market indices in the United States.

It was created by Charles Dow in 1896.

The index tracks 30 large publicly traded companies from different sectors of the U.S. economy.

Unlike the S&P 500, the Dow Jones is a price-weighted index. This means companies with higher stock prices have a greater influence on the index regardless of their market capitalization.

The Dow Jones is commonly used as an indicator of overall market sentiment and economic health.

Examples of companies included in the index include:

- Apple
- Microsoft
- Goldman Sachs
- Visa
- Johnson & Johnson
- Coca-Cola
- Walmart
- Boeing

The composition of the index is periodically reviewed and updated by S&P Dow Jones Indices.
