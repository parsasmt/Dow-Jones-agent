# Dow Jones vs S&P 500

Dow Jones:

- Tracks 30 companies
- Price-weighted
- Represents large established companies

S&P 500:

- Tracks approximately 500 companies
- Market capitalization weighted
- Provides broader market coverage

The S&P 500 is generally considered a better representation of the overall U.S. stock market, while the Dow Jones is often used as a simple indicator of market performance.
