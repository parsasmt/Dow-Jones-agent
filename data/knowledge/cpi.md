# Consumer Price Index (CPI)

The Consumer Price Index measures the average change in prices paid by consumers for a basket of goods and services.

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes CPI monthly.

Investors closely watch CPI because it provides insight into inflation trends.

A CPI reading higher than market expectations often causes stock markets to decline because investors anticipate higher interest rates.

A lower-than-expected CPI may support equity markets by reducing expectations of monetary tightening.
