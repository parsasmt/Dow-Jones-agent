# How the Dow Jones is Calculated

The Dow Jones Industrial Average is calculated using a price-weighted methodology.

The formula is:

Dow Jones = Sum of Stock Prices / Dow Divisor

The Dow Divisor is adjusted whenever stock splits, spin-offs, or changes in index components occur.

Unlike capitalization-weighted indices, the Dow does not consider the size of a company. A company with a higher share price contributes more to the movement of the index.

Because of this methodology, changes in expensive stocks have a larger effect on the index than lower-priced stocks.
