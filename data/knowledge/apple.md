# Apple Inc.

Apple is one of the largest technology companies in the world.

Ticker Symbol: AAPL

Sector: Technology

Apple became a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average in 2015.

Major products include:

- iPhone
- Mac
- iPad
- Apple Watch
- Services

Apple's quarterly earnings reports and product announcements can significantly influence investor sentiment.

Although Apple has one of the largest market capitalizations globally, its influence on the Dow Jones depends on its share price because the index is price-weighted.
