# Inflation

Inflation measures the rate at which prices for goods and services increase over time.

Moderate inflation is normal in a growing economy, but high inflation reduces purchasing power.

Inflation often causes investors to expect higher interest rates from the Federal Reserve.

Higher inflation generally creates downward pressure on stock prices because borrowing becomes more expensive and future corporate earnings are discounted more heavily.

Important inflation indicators include:

- Consumer Price Index (CPI)
- Producer Price Index (PPI)
- Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE)
